# Document design contract

- Preset: `narrative_proposal`
- Header pattern: `editorial_cover`, compact opening-block override
- Page: US Letter, portrait
- Margins: 0.80 in (named `academic_density` override required by the 5–6 page brief)
- Header/footer distance: 0.42 in
- Body: Calibri 9.5 pt, justified, 3 pt after, 1.05 line spacing
- Title: Calibri 23 pt, navy, bold, 0 pt before, 5 pt after
- H1: Calibri 13.5 pt, navy, 9 pt before, 4 pt after
- H2: Calibri 11 pt, teal, 6 pt before, 3 pt after
- Caption/reference: Calibri 8 pt, muted gray, 3 pt before/after
- Lists: avoided in the paper body; architecture definitions use prose and a
  fixed-geometry comparison table.
- Tables: fixed DXA width, 100 DXA indent, 80/80/100/100 DXA cell margins,
  navy header with white text, thin light-gray body grid.
- Page furniture: quiet two-part running header and right-aligned PAGE/NUMPAGES
  footer.
- Colors: navy `#17324D`, teal `#1B7F79`, ink `#20262E`, muted `#5F6B76`,
  light fill `#EEF3F6`.
